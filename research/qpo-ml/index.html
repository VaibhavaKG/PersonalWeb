Research Overview

My current research preparation focuses on quasi-periodic oscillations (QPOs) in astronomical data and the use of machine-learning techniques to investigate physics-driven questions. The specific scientific question and methodology remain under development. My immediate focus is to build the relevant astrophysical, mathematical, statistical, and computational foundations, understand existing literature, and identify a scientifically meaningful direction.

Structure of a Research Paper

Standard structural sequence for a scientific computational and observational astrophysics paper.

1 Title
→
2 Abstract
→
3 Introduction
→
4 Related Work
→
5 Data & Methods
→
6 Results
→
7 Discussion
→
8 Conclusion
→
9 References
Title

Identifies the subject and contribution.

Abstract

Summarises the question, methods, results, and implications.

Introduction

Establishes the scientific context and research question.

Related Work

Explains what previous researchers have done.

Data and Methods

Describes the observations, analysis, models, and validation.

Results

Reports the findings.

Discussion

Interprets the findings, compares them with existing work, and explains limitations.

Conclusion

Summarises what was established and what remains unresolved.

References

Documents the sources used.

Note: The abstract is generally written after the results and conclusions have been established, even though it appears near the beginning of the paper.
