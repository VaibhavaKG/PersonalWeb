Pallavi (Chorus)
Irabeku iruvanthe, toredu saavira chinte One should live just as one is meant to, letting go of a thousand worries, Male surisi haguraada mugilinate... Just like the rain-bearing cloud that feels light after shedding its rain...
Charana 1
Tannodala taaregala gudisi raashiya maadi Just like how the cloud clears the stars from its body, gathers them in a heap, Belakinundeya baanigurulu bittu hege mareyaavudho and releases a ball of light into the sky before fading into the deep and dark night. Nirdhanika nattirulu haage baalisu guruve karuneyittu. Bless me to live such a life, Oh Guru, showering your grace.
Charana 2
Taanu bisilali nintu tanna bali baruvavage Just as how the green-leafed Honge (Indian Beech) tree stands in the scorching sun, yet offers a cool, comforting, sheltering shade to anyone who comes to it; Tannagina aasareya neralu kottu Hege geluvaaguvudho hasireleya honge mara Haage baalisu guruve preetiyittu. Guide me to live like this, Oh Guru, showering your love.
Charana 3
Taanu kesarali kusiyuttiddaru taawareyu Just like how the lotus flower, though rooted in muddy sludge, Maridumbigala poreva thottilaagi becomes a protecting cradle for young bees Hege taayatvanava preetiyali mereyuvudho and wonderfully showcases the essence of motherhood and love; Haage baalisu guruve karuneyittu. Guide me to live like this, Oh Guru, showing your mercy.
Charana 4
Daariyuddakoo pairu naguvante neerunisi Just as how a river nourishes the crops so they smile along her path, Haaluteneyali amruta tumbi nadiyu fills her flowing bounty with the nectar of milk/grain, Hege dooraada neeliyali konegolluvudho and finally merges peacefully into the distant blue ocean; Haage konegaanisu krupeyanittu. Guide me to reach the end of my life gracefully, Oh Guru, by bestowing your grace.
