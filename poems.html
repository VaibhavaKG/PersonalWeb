If you can keep your head when all about you
Are losing theirs and blaming it on you,
If you can trust yourself when all men doubt you,
But make allowance for their doubting too;
If you can wait and not be tired by waiting,
Or being lied about, don’t deal in lies,
Or being hated, don’t give way to hating,
And yet don’t look too good, nor talk too wise:
If you can dream—and not make dreams your master;
If you can think—and not make thoughts your aim;
If you can meet with Triumph and Disaster
And treat those two impostors just the same;
If you can bear to hear the truth you’ve spoken
Twisted by knaves to make a trap for fools,
Or watch the things you gave your life to, broken,
And stoop and build ’em up with worn-out tools:
If you can make one heap of all your winnings
And risk it on one turn of pitch-and-toss,
And lose, and start again at your beginnings
And never breathe a word about your loss;
If you can force your heart and nerve and sinew
To serve your turn long after they are gone,
And so hold on when there is nothing in you
Except the Will which says to them: ‘Hold on!’
If you can talk with crowds and keep your virtue,
Or walk with Kings nor lose the common touch,
If neither foes nor loving friends can hurt you,
If all men count with you, but none too much;
If you can fill the unforgiving minute
With sixty seconds’ worth of distance run,
Yours is the Earth and everything that’s in it,
And, which is more you’ll be a Man, my son!

Pallavi
ಇರಬೇಕು ಇರುವಂತೆ ತೊರೆದು ಸಾವಿರ ಚಿಂತೆ One should live just as one is meant to, letting go of a thousand worries, ಮಳೆ ಸುರಿಸಿ ಹಗುರಾದ ಮುಗಿಲಿನಂತೆ... Just like the rain-bearing cloud that feels light after shedding its rain...
Charana 1
ತನ್ನೊಡಲ ತಾರೆಗಳ ಗುಡಿಸಿ ರಾಶಿಯ ಮಾಡಿ Just like how the cloud clears the stars from its body, gathers them in a heap, ಬೆಳಗಿನುಂಡೆಯ ಬಾನಿಗುರುಳು ಬಿಟ್ಟು ಹೇಗೆ ಮರೆಯಾವುದೋ And releases a ball of light into the sky before fading into the deep, ನಿರ್ಧನಿಕ ನತ್ತಿರುಳು ಹಾಗೆ ಬಾಳಿಸು ಗುರುವೇ ಕರುಣೆಯಿಟ್ಟು. Dark, penniless night; bless me to live such a life, Oh Guru, showering your grace.
Charana 2
ತಾನು ಬಿಸಿಲಲಿ ನಿಂತು ತನ್ನ ಬಳಿ ಬರುವವಗೆ Just as how the green-leafed Honge (Indian Beech) tree stands in the scorching sun, ತಣ್ಣಗಿನ ಆಸರೆಯ ನೆರಳ ಕೊಟ್ಟು ಹೇಗೆ ಗೆಲುವಾಗುವುದೋ Yet offers a cool, comforting, sheltering shade to anyone who comes to it; ಹಸಿರೆಲೆಯ ಹೊಂಗೆ ಮರ ಹಾಗೆ ಬಾಳಿಸು ಗುರುವೇ ಪ್ರೀತಿಯಿಟ್ಟು. Guide me to live like this, Oh Guru, showering your love.
Charana 3
ತಾನು ಕೆಸರಲಿ ಕುಸಿಯುತ್ತಿದ್ದರೂ ತಾವರೆಯು Just like how the lotus flower, though rooted in muddy sludge, ಮರಿದುಂಬಿಗಳ ಪೊರೆವ ತೊಟ್ಟಿಲಾಗಿ ಹೇಗೆ ತಾಯ್ತನವನ್ನು ಪ್ರೀತಿಯಲಿ ಮೆರೆಯುವುದೋ Becomes a protecting cradle for young bees and wonderfully showcases the essence of motherhood and love; ಹಾಗೆ ಬಾಳಿಸು ಗುರುವೇ ಕರುಣೆಯಿಟ್ಟು. Guide me to live like this, Oh Guru, showing your mercy.
Charana 4
ದಾರಿಯುದ್ದಕ್ಕೂ ಪೈರು ನಗುವಂತೆ ನೀರುಣಿಸಿ Just as how a river nourishes the crops so they smile along her path, ಹಾಲುತೆನೆಯಲಿ ಅಮೃತ ತುಂಬಿ ನದಿಯು ಹೇಗೆ ದೂರಾದ ನೀಲಿಯಲಿ ಕೊನೆಗೊಳ್ಳುವುದೋ Fills her flowing bounty with the nectar of milk/grain, and finally merges peacefully into the distant blue ocean; ಹಾಗೆ ಕೊನೆಗಾಣಿಸು ಕೃಪೆಯನ್ನಿಟ್ಟು. Guide me to reach the end of my life gracefully, Oh Guru, by bestowing your grace.

Rathnana Padagalu (ರತ್ನನ ಪದಗಳು) is a celebrated collection of 77 Kannada poems composed by renowned poet G. P. Rajarathnam. Written in the colloquial Bengaluru-Mysuru dialect of common folk, these verses are spoken through the persona of Rathna who's a rustic, unlettered alcoholic. Beneath his drunken candour and humour lies a fierce, unshakeable devotion to the Kannada tongue and an earnest folk philosophy of life, suffering, and contentment.
೧. ಕನ್ನಡ ಪದಗೊಳು (Kannada Padagolu)
ಯೆಂಡ ಯೆಡ್ತಿ ಕನ್ನಡ್ ಪದಗೊಳ್ Toddy, my wife, and Kannada verses— ಅಂದ್ರೆ ರತ್ನಂಗ್ ಪ್ರಾಣ! to Rathna, they are his very breath and life! ಬುಂಡೇನ್ ಎತ್ತಿ ಕುಡದ್ಬುಟ್ಟಾಂಡೆದ್ರೆ- Once I lift the earthen pot and chug down the drink— ತಕ್ಕೊ! ಪದಗೊಳ್ ಬಾಣ! Take that! Verses shoot out like blazing arrows!
ಬಗವಂತ್ ಏನ್ರ ಬೂಮೀಗ್ ಇಳದು If Bhagavantha (God) ever descends down to Earth ನನ್ ತಾಕ್ ಬಂದಾಂತ್ ಅನ್ನು; and happens to come right before me; ಪರ್ ಗಿರೀಕ್ಸೆ ಮಾಡ್ತಾನ್ ಔನು- He might put me through a grueling test— ಬಕ್ತನ್ ಮೇಲ್ ಔನ್ ಕಣ್ಣು! keeping His divine eye on His devotee!
'ಯೆಂಡ ಕುಡಿಯಾದ್ ಬುಟ್ ಬುಡ್ ರತ್ನ!' 'Give up drinking toddy, Rathna!' ಅಂತ್ ಔನ್ ಏನಾರ್ ಅಂದ್ರೆ- if He happens to command me so— ಮೂಗ್ ಮೂರ್ ಚೂರಾಗ್ ಮುರಸ್ಕೋಂತೀನಿ I'd get my nose smashed into three broken pieces ದೇವರ್ ಮಾತ್ಗ್ ಅಡ್ಬಂದ್ರೆ! before I dare disobey the words of God!
'ಯೆಂಡ ಬುಟ್ಟೆ. ಯೆಡ್ತೀನ್ ಬುಟ್ ಬುಡ್!' 'You gave up toddy. Now leave your wife too!' ಅಂತ್ ಔನ್ ಏನಾರ್ ಅಂದ್ರೆ- if He then commands me so— ಕಳದೋಯ್ತ್ ಅಂತ ಕುಣದಾಡ್ತೀನಿ I will dance with absolute joy that she is gone, ದೊಡ್ಡ್ ಒಂದ್ ಕಾಟ! ತೊಂದ್ರೆ! rid of a massive headache and endless trouble!
'ಕನ್ನಡ ಪದಗೊಳ್ ಆಡೋದ್ನೆಲ್ಲ 'All these Kannada verses that you sing,' ನಿಲ್ಲೀಸ್ ಬುಡಬೇಕ್ ರತ್ನ!' 'you must stop singing them at once, Rathna!' ಅಂತ್ ಔನ್ ಅಂದ್ರೆ - ದೇವ್ರ್ ಆದ್ರ್ ಏನು! if He dares say that—I don't care if He is God! ಮಾಡ್ತೀನ್ ಔನ್ಗೆ ಖತ್ನ! I will slice Him down and finish Him off!
ಆಗ್ನೆ ಮಾಡೋ ಐಗೋಳ್ ಎಲ್ಲಾ All those big shots who issue grand orders, ದೇವ್ರೆ ಆಗ್ಲಿ - ಎಲ್ಲ! let them even be gods—every single one! ಕನ್ನಡ್ ಸುದ್ದೀಗ್ ಏನ್ರ ಬಂದ್ರೆ If anyone ever dares mess with Kannada, ಮಾನಾ ಉಳಸಾಕಿಲ್ಲ! I swear I will not spare their honor or life!
ನರಕಕ್ಕ್ ಇಳ್ಸಿ ನಾಲ್ಗೆ ಸೀಳ್ಸಿ Even if they drag me down to Hell and slit my tongue, ಬಾಯ್ ಒಲಿಸಾಕಿದ್ರೂನೆ- even if they stitch my mouth completely shut— ಮೂಗ್ನಲ್ ಕನ್ನಡ್ ಪದವಾಡ್ತೀನಿ! I will sing Kannada verses through my nostrils! ನನ್ ಮನಸನ್ನ್ ನೀ ಕಾಣೆ! You have no idea what my heart is made of!
ಯೆಂಡ ಓಗ್ಲಿ! ಯೆಡ್ತಿ ಓಗ್ಲಿ! Let toddy go! Let my wife go! ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಕೊಚ್ಕೊಂಡ್ ಓಗ್ಲಿ! Let everything be washed away and lost! ಪರ್ಪಂಚ್ ಇರೋ ತನಕ ಮುಂದೆ As long as this wide universe exists ahead, ಕನ್ನಡ್ ಪದಗೊಳ್ ನುಗ್ಲಿ! let Kannada verses surge boldly forward!
೨. ರತ್ನನ್ ಪರ್ಪಂಚ (Rathnan Parpancha)
ಯೇಳ್ಕೊಳ್ಳಾಕ್ ಒಂದ್ ಊರು A hometown to call my own, ತಲೇಮೇಗ್ ಒಂದ್ ಸೂರು a roof over my head, ಮಲಗಾಕೆ ಬೂಮ್ತಾಯಿ ಮಂಚ; Mother Earth as the bed to sleep on; ಕೈ ಯಿಡದೋಳ್ ಪುಟ್ನಂಜಿ Putnanji, the woman whose hand I held in marriage, ನೆಗನೆಗತ ಉಪ್ಗಂಜಿ smiling warmly as she serves me salted rice porridge— ಕೊಟ್ರಾಯ್ತು ರತ್ನನ್ ಪರ್ಪಂಚ! that's more than enough for Rathna's whole universe!
ಅಗಲೆಲ್ಲ ಬೆವರ್ ಅರ್ಸಿ Toiling all day long, pouring out sweat, ತಂದದ್ರಲ್ಲ್ ಒಸಿ ಮುರ್ಸಿ sparing a little change from what was earned, ಸಂಜೇಲಿ ವುಳಿ ಯೆಂಡ ಕೊಂಚ a tiny cup of sour toddy in the twilight evening, ಯೀರ್ತ ಮೈ ಝುಂ ಅಂದ್ರೆ as I sip it and my body begins to tingle, ವಾಸ್ನೆ ಘಂ ಘಂ ಅಂದ್ರ್ಎ and the heady aroma wafts sweetly around— ತುಭೋಯ್ತು ರತ್ನನ್ ಪರ್ಪಂಚ! Rathna's universe is filled with total bliss!
ಏನೋ ಕುಸಿಯಾದಾಗ Whenever a strange joy bubbles within, ಮತ್ತ್ ಎಚ್ಚಿ ಓದಾಗ and the drunkenness rises and overflows, ಅಂಗೇನೆ ಪರಪಂಚದ್ ಅಂಚ just like that, crossing beyond the very rim of the universe, ದಾಟ್ಕಂಡಿ ಆರಾಡ್ತ soaring high and flying free, ಕನ್ನಡದಲ್ ಪದವಾಡ್ತ singing verses in pure Kannada, ಇಗ್ಗೋದು ರತ್ನನ್ ಪರ್ಪಂಚ! that is how Rathna's universe rejoices and thrives!
ದುಕ್ಕಿಲ್ಲ ದಾಲಿಲ್ಲ No sorrow, no burning grief, ನಮಗ್ ಅದರಾಗ್ ಪಾಲಿಲ್ಲ we have no share in all that misery; ನಾವ್ ಕಂಡಿಲ್ಲ್ ಆ ತಂಚ ವಂಚ; we have never seen deceit, trickery, or fraud; ನಮ್ಮಸ್ಟಕ್ ನಾವಾಗಿ minding our own humble selves, ಇದ್ದಿದ್ರಲ್ಲ್ ಆಯಾಗಿ living in peace and contentment with whatever we have— ಬಾಳೋದು ರತ್ನನ್ ಪರ್ಪಂಚ! that is how Rathna's universe lives on!
’ಬಡತನ ಗಿಡತನ 'Poverty and lack, whatever it may be— ಏನಿದ್ರೇನ್ ? ನಡೆತೇನ what does it matter? Keeping one's conduct ಚೆಂದಾಗ್ ಇಟ್ಕೊಳ್ಳಾದೆ ಅಚ್ಛ!’ - pure and upright is what is truly beautiful!' ಅಂದ್ಕೊಂಡಿ ಸುಕವಾಗಿ believing so with a peaceful soul, ಕಸ್ಟಕ್ ನೆಗಮೊಕವಾಗಿ facing every hardship with a smiling face— ನೆಗೆಯೋದೆ ರತ್ನನ್ ಪರ್ಪಂಚ! laughing through it all is Rathna's universe!
ದೇವ್ರ್ ಏನ್ರ ಕೊಡಲಣ್ಣ Whether God grants us anything, brother, ಕೊಡದಿದ್ರೆ ಬುಡಲಣ್ಣ or gives us nothing at all, so be it; ನಾವೆಲ್ಲ ಔನೀಗೆ ಬಚ್ಚ! before Him, we are all just little children! ಔನ್ ಆಕಿದ್ ತಾಳ್ದಂಗೆ To the rhythm and beat He plays, ಕಣ್ ಮುಚ್ಚೊಂಡ್ ಯೇಳ್ದಂಗೆ with eyes closed, following wherever He leads— ಕುಣಿಯಾದೆ ರತ್ನನ್ ಪರ್ಪಂಚ! dancing to His tune is Rathna's universe!
೩. ಮೊಟ್ಮೊದಲು (Motmodalu)
ನನಗೂನೆ ಯೆಂಡಕ್ಕು ಬಲ್ ಬಲೆ ದೋಸ್ತಿ. Between me and toddy, there is a tremendous, unbreakable friendship. ಕುಡದ್ ಬುಟ್ಟಾಗ್ ಆಡೋದು ನಂಗ್ ಪೂರ ಜಾಸ್ತಿ. Whenever I drink, my tongue just cannot stop singing verses. ನಂಗ್ ಎಸರು ಯೇಳ್ತಾರೆ - ರ್ರರ್ರರ್ರರ್ರತ್ನ. People call out my name as—R-r-r-r-Rathna! ನಾನ್ ಆಡೋ ಪದಗೋಳು ಯೆಂಡದ್ ಪ್ರಯತ್ನ. All these verses I sing are really the doings of toddy. ಮಾಬಾರ್ತ ಬರೆಯಾಕೆ ಯಾಸಂಗ್ ಇನಾಯ್ಕ Just like how Vyasa found Vinayaka (Ganesha) to write the Mahabharata, ಸಿಕ್ದಂಗೆ ನಂಗ್ ಒಬ್ಬ ಬೇವಾರ್ಸಿನಾಯ್ಕ I too bumped into a jobless fellow of a scoundrel, ಸಿಕ್ಕೋನೆ, ನನ್ ಆಡ್ನ ಕೂಡಿಸ್ದ ಬರ್ದು! who sat down and dutifully penned down every song I sang! ಏನ್ ಐತೊ ಯಾರ್ ಬಲ್ರು ಔಂಗ್ ಇರೊ ದರ್ದು! Who knows what crazy itch or agony was driving him! ’ಬರಕೊಂಡ್ರೆ ಬರಕೊಂಡ್ ಓಗ್, ನಿಂಗೂನೆ ಐಲು; 'If you want to write it down, go right ahead, you're crazy too; ಆದಸ್ಟೂ ಮಾಡಾನೆ ಸಾಯ ನಂಕೈಲು’ I'll sing as much as I can till I die!' ಅಂದ್ಕಂಡ್ ಔನ್ ಬರದಿದ್ನ ಅಚ್ಗ್ ಆಕೋಕ್ ಒಪ್ಪಿ Thinking so, I agreed to have whatever he scribbled sent to print, ಕಳಿಸಿವ್ನಿ. ಬೈದೀರ ನನಗೇನ್ರ ತಪ್ಪಿ! and sent it off. Please don't curse or scold me if there are errors! ಅಕ್ಸಾರ ಗಿಕ್ಸಾರ ನನಗೇನೂ ಬರ್ದು. I know nothing of alphabets, letters, or reading. (ದೊಡ್ ಚಾಕ್ರಿ ಬೇಕಂದ್ರೆ ಓದೆ ಬೇಕು ದರ್ದು!) (If you want a high-paying job, you must suffer the headache of schooling!) ಪದಗೋಳು ಚಂದ್ ಇದ್ರೆ ಯೆಂಡಕ್ ಸಿಪಾರ್ಸಿ! If the verses sound sweet, credit goes to the toddy! ಚಂದಾಗ್ ಇಲ್ದಿದ್ರನಕ ತಪ್ಗೆ ಬೇವಾರ್ಸಿ! If they sound terrible, blame that good-for-nothing scribe!
೪. ಕುಡಕರ್ ಮಾತ್ವ (Kudakar Maathwa)
ಕುಡಕರ್ ಮಾತ್ವ ತಿಳಿಕೊಳ್ದೇನೆ Without truly understanding the philosophy of drunkards, ನೂಕ್ಬಾರ್ದ್ ಔರ್ನ ಕೆಳಗೆ; you must not push them down in contempt; ಯಾವ್ ಚಿಪ್ನಾಗ್ ಮುತ್ತ್ ಐತೊ- Which oyster shell holds the precious pearl within— ಒಡದ್ ನೋಡ್ಬೇಕ್ ಒಳಗೆ! you must break it open to look deep inside!
ಕೊಚ್ಚೆ ನೀರೀನ್ ಸೋದೀಸ್ತ್ ಅಂದ್ರೆ If you filter and purify muddy gutter water, ಸಿಕ್ಕೋಕಿಲ್ವ ಗಂಗೆ? won't the pure essence of Ganga be found? ಸಾಜಾ ಯೇಳೋನ್ ಯಾರಾದ್ರೇನು? What does it matter who speaks the plain truth, ಸತ್ಯ ಕಣ್ ಕಂಡೌಂಗೆ! to the one whose eyes have truly seen reality!
ಆರ್ತ್ ಇಲ್ಲಾಂತ್ ನೆಗಬೇಡಾಣ್ಣ Don't laugh at me, brother, thinking there's no meaning ನಾಕುಡದಾಡೋ ಮಟ್ಟು! in the drunken tunes and meters that I sing! ಕುಡಕನ್ ಪದಗೊಳ್ ಒಕ್ಕ್ ನೋಡಿದ್ರೆ If you thresh and sift through a drunkard's verses, ಮಸ್ತಾಗ್ ಅವೆ ಗುಟ್ಟು! you will find an abundance of deep, sacred secrets!
’ರವ್ವಿ ಕಾಣದ್ ಕವಿ ಕಂಡ’ 'What the Sun could not see, the Poet perceived'— ಅಂದ್ರೆ ಕವಿಗೊಳ್ ತತ್ವ- so goes the celebrated philosophy of poets; ’ಕವ್ವಿ ಕಾಣದ್ ಕುಡಕ ಕಂಡ’ 'What even the Poet could not see, the Drunkard perceived'— ಅನ್ನೊದ್ ಕುಡಕರ್ ಮಾತ್ವ! that is the supreme philosophy of drunkards!
೫. ರತ್ನನ್ ಯೋಚ್ನೆ (Rathnan Yochne)
ಬಯಸಿದ ಸಾಮನ್ ಬೇಕಾದಂಗೆ Whether the things one desired ಇರಲಿ ಇಲ್ದೇನ್ ಇರಲಿ- are within reach or not at all— ತಾಪತ್ರೇಯನಕ್ ತಪ್ಪಿದ್ದಲ್ಲ! worry and vexation cannot be escaped! ಸುಳ್ಳಂತ್ ಅನ್ನೌನ್ ಬರಲಿ! Let anyone who claims this is a lie step forward!
ಬುಂಡೇಲ್ ಒಂದ್ ತೊಟ್ ಇರೊಗಂಟ್ಲೂನೆ As long as there remains even a single drop in the pot, ನಿದ್ದೆ ಗಿದ್ದೆ ಬರದು! sleep will not come at all! ಬುಂಡೇ ಕಾಲಿ ಆಗೋದ್ರನಕ Until the entire pot is emptied out, ನಿದ್ದೆ ಬರಲೇ ಬರದು! sleep will never, ever arrive!
ಈ ತಾಪತ್ರೇನ್ ತಳ್ಳಾಕಾಕೆ To push away and escape this ceaseless worry, ಏನ್ ಮಾಡ್ಬೇಕೊ ಕಾಣೆ! I know not what on earth should be done! ಯೋಚಿಸ್ದಸೂ ತಗಲ್ಕೋಂತೈತ್ ಆ The more I think about it, the deeper it entangles, ಯೋಚ್ನೇಗ್ ಒಂದ್ ಒಸ್ ಸಾಣೆ! giving my troubled thoughts another sharp whetting edge!
ಬಯಸಿದ್ ಸಾಮಾನ್ ಬೇಕಾದಂಗೆ Whether the things one desired ಇರಲಿ ಇಲ್ದೇನ್ ಇರಲಿ- are within reach or not at all— ತಾಪತ್ರೇಯನಕ್ ತಪ್ಪಿದ್ದಲ್ಲ! worry and vexation cannot be escaped! ಸುಳ್ಳಂತ್ ಅನ್ನೌನ್ ಬರಲಿ. Let anyone who claims this is a lie step forward.
